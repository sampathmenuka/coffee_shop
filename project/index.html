Our story

Three generations, one perfect cup

Founded in 1952, COFFEE began as a six-stool counter on Coffee Street. Our legacy is built on quality beans, expert brewing, and a passion for bringing people together over a perfect cup.

Today we still roast every bean in-house, work directly with growers across three continents, and greet regulars by name.

  • 1952First counter opens on Coffee Street
  • 1978In-house roastery fires up
  • 2004Direct-trade partnerships begin
  • TodayStill family-run, still roasting daily
